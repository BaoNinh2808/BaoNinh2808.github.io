About

I am a cryptography researcher at Orochi Network. I work on post-quantum privacy-preserving protocols. My current focus is single-server private information retrieval (PIR) from lattices (LWE): making it concretely efficient, and letting it keep up with a database that changes over time without costly re-preprocessing. I am also interested in fully homomorphic encryption and in verifiable computation, especially zkSNARKs that combine verifiability with privacy.

I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology (Honors Program) from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, in 2025.
