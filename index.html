Cryptography Researcher · Orochi Network

I am a cryptography researcher at Orochi Network. I work on post-quantum privacy-preserving protocols. My current focus is single-server private information retrieval (PIR) from lattices (LWE): making it concretely efficient, and letting it keep up with a database that changes over time without costly re-preprocessing. I am also interested in fully homomorphic encryption and in verifiable computation, especially zkSNARKs that combine verifiability with privacy. I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology (Honors Program) from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, in 2025.

Research

Selected lines of work.

RisePIR: incremental keyword PIR

A generic construction of incremental keyword PIR from any updatable index PIR, built on a new d-ary Segmented Cuckoo Filter and instantiated from LWE. Deployed as a private Ethereum eth_getBalance service: over all 205 million non-zero mainnet accounts, a private query takes 0.69 s and each new block is absorbed in 5.6 ms.

paper · code · demo

Fully verifiable attribute-based encryption

A CP-ABE scheme that stays sound against a dishonest key authority or encryptor. Users can verify that their keys match their attributes, and anyone can check that a ciphertext is well formed under its declared policy, using pairing-based techniques.

paper

Zero-knowledge proofs in practice

At Orochi Network, I moved the zkDatabase provable database from Kimchi to Groth16, cutting proving time by about 50×. I also co-authored a technical report on Circle STARKs over the Mersenne-31 field.
