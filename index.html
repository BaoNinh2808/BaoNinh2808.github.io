About

I am a cryptography researcher at Orochi Network. I work on post-quantum privacy-preserving protocols. My current focus is single-server private information retrieval (PIR) from lattices (LWE): making it concretely efficient, and letting it keep up with a database that changes over time without costly re-preprocessing. I am also interested in fully homomorphic encryption and in verifiable computation, especially zkSNARKs that combine verifiability with privacy.

I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology (Honors Program) from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, in 2025.

§1Research

At Orochi Network (since 2025) I designed RisePIR, an LWE-based incremental keyword PIR built on a new d-ary Segmented Cuckoo Filter (CANS 2026). It powers a private Ethereum eth_getBalance service (risepir.org): over all 205 million non-zero mainnet accounts, a private query takes 0.69 s and each new block is absorbed in 5.6 ms. The code is open source.

I also work on zero-knowledge proofs in practice. I moved the zkDatabase provable database from Kimchi to Groth16, cutting proving time by about 50×, and co-authored a technical report on Circle STARKs over the Mersenne-31 field.

Before that, as an undergraduate research assistant at the Computer Network Lab, University of Science, VNU-HCM (2024–2025), I built a fully verifiable CP-ABE scheme (CANS 2025) that stays sound against a dishonest key authority or encryptor.
